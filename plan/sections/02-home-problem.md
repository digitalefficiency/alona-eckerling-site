0. **רציפות (arrives→leaves)**

LAYER 0 — CONTINUITY (arrives → leaves). She lands here straight out of the Hero, where she just read the common-ground promise ('את כבר יודעת מה לאכול… מה שחסר זה לא עוד תפריט אלא דרך… כדי שסוף סוף יהיה שקט') beside the small R.D. credential token. She arrives CURIOUS but GUARDED — 'אולי הפעם שונה' — still keeping her arm out, half-expecting one more diet pitch. This section is the first full stop after the hero's soft reveal: the motion quiets, the page narrows to a single reading column, and the tone drops from 'promise' to 'I see you'. The IN-handoff is emotional permission — the hero made a claim, and the very first job of Problem is to prove the claim knows her by naming her week in her own words before asking for anything. The OUT-handoff must leave her having thought, involuntarily, 'זאת בדיוק אני' — shoulders lowered, first exhale, guard softened from 'prove it' to 'okay, go on'. Critically, the section closes not on the wound but on the opening line of relief ('את לא צריכה לעשות את זה לבד… שיהיה שקט'), which is the exact door the NEXT section (03 Guide) walks through: the reader is now primed to meet the real human + the R.D. license as the answer to the loneliness this section just named. Nothing here resolves the yo-yo or proves authority — those are later beats; this section's only currency is recognition, and it must hand the reader to the Guide already leaning in.

1. **ביט ותפקיד (הכאב שהוא משרת)**

LAYER 1 — BEAT & JOB (the pain it serves). Beat = TENSION, structured as textbook PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution-seed), and it is the section that OPENS the home-only 'noise → quiet' signature motif. It carries four pains from the STORY hierarchy in deliberate order. SPINE = Pain #1, the ANCHOR knowledge-action-gap (weight 15): 'אני יודעת בדיוק מה לאכול, אני פשוט לא מצליחה לעשות את זה לבד, וניסיתי הכל' — this owns the Problem naming and becomes the lifted PullQuote. ENGINE = Pain #2, food-guilt-noise / שקט (weight 12, her single most differentiated axis) — it powers the Agitate paragraph (the evening mental noise, counting bites, the guilt over a cookie) AND it is the literal seed of the noise→quiet motif that pays off at Success (section 08). LOOP = Pain #3, yo-yo fear (weight 12): named lightly as 'לזמן מה זה עובד, ואז הכל מתפרק שוב' / 'וזה מתחיל מהתחלה' — present as tension only, never resolved here. RELIEF SEED = Pain #4, loneliness (weight 8): the Solution sentence 'את לא צריכה לעשות את זה לבד', which justifies the human 1:1 and hands off to the Guide. Appearance/body-image stays MUTED (YMYL): no kilos, no before/after, no event-shame — the ache is mental noise and self-blame, not a number on a scale. Authority-doubt (Pain #5) and no-time (Pain #6) are deliberately NOT touched here; they belong to Guide and Plan. The job is singular: convert guarded curiosity into felt recognition, and light the motif.

2. **הרגש המדויק (לפני→אחרי)**

LAYER 2 — EXACT EMOTION (before → after, felt words). BEFORE (what she carries in): exhausted, guilty, quietly cynical, self-blaming ('אין לי כוח רצון'), braced for another pitch, tired of hearing 'just eat less', lonely in it, half-hoping and hating that she still hopes. The dominant body-feeling is a clenched, over-full head — noise. AFTER (what this section must install by its last line): seen, named, understood without being judged; the specific relief of 'someone described the inside of my evening and didn't blame me for it'; shoulders dropping, a first real exhale, the sting of self-blame loosening into 'oh — it was never the knowledge, and it was never my willpower'; cautious, warming hope; the very first taste of the word that defines the whole brand — שקט (quiet). The transition verb is RELAX, not CONVINCE: she does not yet believe the solution works, she simply stops defending herself. Forbidden after-states (YMYL + brand): fear, urgency, shame, being 'called out', or feeling sold to. If she leaves feeling exposed rather than held, the section has failed. Peer-close register throughout ('את'), the sane calm voice in the noisy room.

3. **מיקרו-נרטיב**

LAYER 3 — MICRO-NARRATIVE (the ~8-second lived sequence). (1) The wide hero motion settles; the viewport narrows to one warm, centered reading column on the paper-grain field — a visual 'the room got quieter'. (2) In the generous margins around the column, faint scattered diet-noise words sit in muted slate-navy at low opacity ('קלוריות', 'אסור', 'אשמה', 'מחר מתחילה מחדש') — she registers them peripherally as the clutter she lives in, but they never touch the readable text. (3) A small muted kicker ('זה מוכר לך?') gives permission; the Problem paragraph fades up line by line and she reads her own week: tried everything, works for a while, then falls apart. (4) The Agitate paragraph lands the evening — counting bites, arguing with herself, guilt over a cookie, the promise that tomorrow will be different — and her guard drops because nobody usually names the INSIDE of it. (5) The oversized PullQuote rises out of its mask in her own first-person voice ('אני יודעת בדיוק מה לאכול. אני פשוט לא מצליחה לעשות את זה לבד.') — the recognition spike, 'זאת בדיוק אני'. (6) The Solution-seed paragraph arrives quiet and short: it was never the knowledge, never the willpower; what's missing is a way built around your real week, and someone beside you — you don't have to do this alone, and it can finally be quiet. (7) As she scrolls past, the margin noise-words begin to drift inward and dim — the motif's first movement — leaving the column, and her head, a little more still. She exhales and keeps reading into the Guide.

7. **הוכחה**

LAYER 7 — PROOF (what earns belief here, and the ethics guardrails). This is an empathy/tension beat, so its proof is NOT credential or testimonial proof — those belong to Guide (03), Proof (05) and stay DARK until real. The proof mechanism here is VOC-RECOGNITION-AS-EVIDENCE: every line is lifted from the real avatar language and the questionnaire (Michal's 'I know what to eat but can't do it alone', the evening guilt-loop, 'tried everything'), so the reader's own involuntary 'זאת בדיוק אני' becomes the credibility event — accuracy of description is the receipt. The one forward-looking claim the section makes ('את לא צריכה לעשות את זה לבד') is honest because it is backed by the genuine human 1:1 offer delivered later in Plan (real dietitian + WhatsApp between sessions + food-diary feedback), not by hype. ETHICS (YMYL, non-negotiable): reassurance-not-fear — the Agitate paragraph names pain but the section MUST close on the relief seed in the same scroll, never leaving her parked in the wound or in urgency; NO invented metrics, NO fabricated testimonial, and the hypothetical ~10kg success story from the questionnaire must NOT appear here in any form; NO before/after or body-shame; register stays non-pathologizing and peer-close. The credential trust-token is deliberately absent (it would break the empathy voice) and is carried by the Hero and the Guide instead. Do not overclaim a 'method' here — the mechanism is only seeded ('דרך שנבנית סביב השבוע שלך'), proven later.
