# 01-home-hero — בית · hero
> ביט: HOOK · archetype: `full-bleed-hero` · רכיב: ImageHero + MOrchestrate (kicker→RevealHeading→CTA)

0. **רציפות (arrives→leaves)**

LAYER 0 — CONTINUITY (arrives → leaves). This is beat 1 of the entire site: nothing renders before it, so it must catch Michal/Noa/Shira mid-scroll-of-life, arriving cold from an Instagram tap, a Google search, or a friend's link. She arrives exhausted from the struggle, guilty, skeptical, half-braced for another 26-year-old influencer pitch, guard fully up and thumb ready to leave. The hero's only continuity job is to lower that guard by exactly one notch and hand her downward, not to close. She leaves curious ('maybe this time is different'), still keeping distance, with one seed of trust planted (the license token) and one honest exit already offered (the recipes off-ramp for not-yet-ready Noa). The out-state feeds directly into section 2 (Problem/tension): the recognition she feels here ('she gets it, she's not telling me to eat less') is what lets the Problem section land its 'this is exactly me.' Two hand-offs leave this section: the dominant vertical scroll into Problem, and a quiet lateral link to /מתכונים for the product-aware buyer who is not ready for a call. Nothing this section promises (quiet in the head, no giving up food, a result that stays, a real licensed R.D.) may go unkept later in the spine.

1. **ביט ותפקיד (הכאב שהוא משרת)**

LAYER 1 — BEAT & JOB (pain served). Beat: HOOK, the opening of the home spine. It OWNS the hero and OPENS the Problem. Primary pain served: pain #1, the ANCHOR (acuity x frequency weight 15), the knowledge-action gap, 'I know what to eat but I can't do it alone, and I've tried everything' (all three avatars A, B, C). The hook line names the knowledge half ('you already know what to eat') so the reader feels validated instead of lectured, and the lede answers the alone/tried-everything half via the mechanism (a way built around your real week, with a licensed R.D. beside you one-on-one). It also lightly pre-loads pain #2 (שקט / food-guilt-noise, weight 12) by naming the promised end-state 'שקט בראש', and pain #3 (yoyo-fear, weight 12) by promising 'התוצאה תישאר'. Concrete objection work done here: it disarms 'I already know what to eat' (A, C) head-on, and it pre-empts 'influencer or real service? / 26, is she qualified?' (C, pain #5) with the checkable R.D. + Ministry-of-Health token beside the CTA. What this beat must NOT do: it does not carry the full Guide/authority argument, the 3-step Plan, or the proof archive; it only plants their seeds. Task job restated for the builder: stop the doubt on the anchor pain, state the common-ground promise, put the R.D. trust-token next to 'בואי נדבר', and give Noa a soft recipes CTA.

2. **הרגש המדויק (לפני→אחרי)**

LAYER 2 — EXACT EMOTION (before → after felt words). BEFORE (the felt state she carries in, in her own register): exhausted, guilty, 'here we go again', cynical and a little burned, braced-for-disappointment, noise-in-the-head, quietly ashamed that she 'knows everything' and still can't do it. AFTER (the felt state this section must produce inside 4 to 6 seconds): a small physical exhale first (the calm image down-regulates her before she reads a word), then recognition ('oh, she gets it, this isn't a diet being sold to me'), then a preview of relief ('שקט' named as a real possibility), then a cautious credibility flip ('wait, she's actually licensed, this might be real') that specifically softens the skeptic. The emotional delta is deliberately small and honest: from braced to slightly-open, from guarded-cynic to cautiously-curious. It is NOT from skeptic to believer, and it is NOT excitement or hype. The two target peak feelings are RECOGNITION ('this is me') and RELIEF-PREVIEW ('quiet is possible'). Forbidden emotions to trigger: shame, body-anxiety, urgency, FOMO, or the sense of being sold to. If the reader feels pressure or fear here, the section has failed its YMYL and brand-soul contract.

She exits with her guard down one notch, either scrolling into Problem or clicking softly toward the recipes.

5. **art-direction — ה-SHOT**

LAYER 5 — ART-DIRECTION SHOT (full-bleed-hero + emotion metaphor + YMYL pixel rules). EMOTION METAPHOR: 'quiet' rendered as unhurried natural light and a dietitian who actually cooks; the frame should feel like a slow morning, not a campaign. SHOT: full-bleed background still of a light-filled warm kitchen; a wooden board on a warm counter with whole fresh vegetables and herbs, a linen cloth, a ceramic bowl, and a woman's hands (cropped at the forearm, mid-gentle-preparation), soft window light from the left. No recognizable face and no body silhouette, because the real portrait is not yet delivered and the direction requires Alona's portrait to always be real. COMPOSITION / RTL: text column anchored to the RIGHT (RTL reading start); keep the right third as calmer negative space (softer focus, lighter counter) so the display text sits over the quiet side; visual interest (produce, hands) weighted to the left. SCRIM: a warm ivory (#F4EDE4 to transparent) gradient from the right edge inward on desktop, tuned so navy #22304C body/H1 text holds at least 4.5:1 contrast over it; a faint warm paper-grain overlay at ~4 to 6% opacity unifies the photo with the #FBF6F1 page. RESPONSIVE: desktop is full-bleed ~16:9/3:2 with the text overlaid on the right scrim; MOBILE stacks for guaranteed AA — a 4:5 crop of the same still on top, then the kicker/H1/lede/CTA on solid warm paper below (no risky text-over-photo on small screens). Soft radii on the CTA pill and chip (pill CTA, 16px on any card). SWAP SLOT: build the image as a single replaceable asset so the client's real hero banner (2500x1330, requested) or a real portrait (4:5) can drop in later without layout change; when a real portrait arrives, it may become an inset 4:5 co-primary on the left. YMYL / FORBIDDEN PIXELS: no before/after, no scales, no measuring tape, no calorie or macro numbers on screen, no thin-body or weight-loss imagery, no plated 'diet portions', no invented media/collab logos, no stock-looking fake smiles. Real bright natural-light food is the proof engine; everything reads warm, calm, and non-restrictive.

6. **תנועה (טוקנים)**

LAYER 6 — MOTION (tokens only, MOrchestrate sequence + reduced-motion twin). Component stack: ImageHero wraps an MOrchestrate parent that staggers its children in reading order. Sequence and tokens: (1) image resolves from an LQIP blur with an ultra-slow settle (scale 1.03 to 1.00, opacity 0 to 1) over var(--dur-settle, ~1000ms) using var(--ease-quiet, cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)); (2) kicker MFadeUp, translateY var(--space-fade, 12px) to 0 over var(--dur-reveal, ~600ms); (3) H1 via RevealHeading/SplitText line-mask, two lines each wiped up behind a clip mask, staggered by var(--stagger-line, ~110ms), duration var(--dur-reveal); (4) lede MFadeUp; (5) CTA row (pill + license chip + micro sub-label) MFadeUp last, so trust arrives with the ask. Global stagger between orchestrate children var(--stagger-2, ~90ms), single soft cascade, total in-view time under ~1.4s so it never feels animated-for-its-own-sake. This section is low-motion by mandate: NO parallax, NO scroll-scrub, NO ken-burns loop; the signature 'noise settles to quiet' motif belongs to Problem/Success, not here (the hero gets 'one soft reveal' only). REDUCED-MOTION STATIC TWIN (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce): all elements render at final opacity 1 with no transform, the image loads at scale 1.00 with a simple opacity fade only (or instant), SplitText renders as plain static headings; the layout and contrast are identical so nothing is lost.

The load-bearing proof object is the checkable credential token beside the CTA: 'דיאטנית קלינית מוסמכת · R.D. · רישיון משרד הבריאות', which does the skeptic's (Shira's) first reassurance that this is a real regulated professional and not an influencer. It is checkable by design (links to /עליי where the license, B.Sc, and Ichilov internship are detailed); the license number 204526-11 is her stated credential and must be printed only after the certificate is verified before launch, and is kept OFF the hero to stay honest. Secondary proof is the specificity of the mechanism itself ('built around your real week', 'without giving up the food you love', 'quiet that stays'), which reads as competence and a point of view rather than a slogan. Tertiary proof is the soft recipes link, a low-commitment preview of proof-of-craft (a dietitian who actually cooks). PROHIBITED here under YMYL and the real-proof-only ethic: no testimonials (the testimonial slot stays dark until real), no invented metrics, no before/after, and absolutely no use of the hypothetical ~10kg success story as if it were a real client. Verification dependencies the builder must respect: a human face may appear only once a real Alona portrait is delivered; the license number appears only after certificate check; media/collab logos stay hidden until approved.
